### How standings are computed (server)
- Member bets = their synced `bets` (sync.js row) merged with the ledger. A bet counts if: its day (America/Chicago; kickoff from ESPN when the game id is known) is inside the group window and after the member joined; verified-only passes; its type is on (Kalshi single markets: ticker SPREAD → spread, TOTAL → totals, GAME → moneyline, else prop); parlays have at most N legs; sport fits (port of the app's v213 bSport: `lg`, Kalshi ticker, else NFL abbreviation list, else college — CAVEAT: a college team with an NFL abbreviation such as MIA counts as NFL unless the bet has `lg`; legs with no teams (e.g. Kalshi combo legs) count only in "Both" groups or next to a matching known leg); a hand-logged bet (`b<ms>` id) created more than 5 minutes after its kickoff never counts.
- Result: `cash` (Kalshi sale) > `fin` > prop `res` (void = push). P/L: cash − stake, else `finPL`, else stake × payout. Cap scales P/L by min(stake, cap) / stake. Units = P/L per $1 staked (won), −1 (lost), 0 (push). Return % = winnings / counted stake. Win % = W / (W + L). Rank by the chosen metric, tiebreak Return %, then name.
- Weeks run Tuesday to Monday (an NFL week); week 1 = start day to the first Monday. Best five = only each member's five best results per week (by P/L, or units for Units / Win %). Min bets: a member with fewer counted bets (settled or open) in the current week is flagged; a short week cannot win the weekly high score.
- G6: `/groups/player` for someone else returns only counted bets whose every leg has kicked off (known ESPN kickoff, else the day is over, or the bet is settled with no future leg); the rest are a count ("N bets are hidden until their games kick off"). Standings carry no bet text at all.
- Start: the phone picks the first kickoff it knows on/after the chosen moment ("Next kickoff", "This weekend", "Next week", a date; fallback a fixed time); the server checks it is in the future and within 400 days, and works out the end ("This weekend" = the Monday after, N weeks, rest of season = Jan 11 2027 NFL / Jan 20 2027 college/both, through the playoffs = Feb 15 2027, or a custom last day). Rules lock at that start; bets count from it.

### Rules as built
- First-seen = the earlier of the server's clock and the member's synced row `updated` (stamped by sync.js on the server). A bet first seen at or after its (first) kickoff never counts. The tick reads members' rows every 5 minutes in game windows (30 otherwise) so bets are seen before kickoff even if nobody opens Groups.
- The snapshot follows edits made before kickoff; from kickoff on it is frozen (`z`). Results come only from the server grade; the phone's won/lost is used only when the contest has "Count bets the app can't check (honor system)" on.
- Visibility: every leg's kickoff known -> shown once the LAST leg kicked off (parlays too, even after a leg lost); any kickoff unknown -> shown only once graded settled. Not-counted bets of others are never sent. Open counts are sent (count only).
- Ranking: members meeting "fewest bets to place in a paid spot" first, then the metric, then Return %; still tied = same place and the places' money split evenly in cents (extra cent to the higher place). Only members meeting the minimum and with a settled bet are owed money.
- Money: main pot = entries of main-pot contests x members marked paid, commissioner's take off the top, then shared between main-pot contests (even / 50-50 / 40-60 / 60-40 / custom). Side pots: own entry, take, opt-ins, paid marks. Locks: each contest at its own start; money rules once anyone else joined or asked.
- Limits: a group's cap = min(its "most people" setting, maxPeople(creator)). Joins, approvals and requests are checked inside the conditional write, so a race fills one seat only.

### Auction (server is the clock)
- Start opens the first lot (random order drawn at start if chosen). The timer (10/20/30 s) starts at the first bid and resets on every bid; bids need >= lowest bid, then high + step (whole dollars); the high bidder cannot raise themselves; a bid for a lot not on the block = "stale"; after the bell = "Sold".
- Lazy clock: any read or write past `endsAt` sells to the high bidder at `endsAt`; the next lot opens 8 s after a sale (or at once with Next lot). Pause freezes the time left (and the 8 s gap); Resume restores it. Undo = last bid on the lot, else reopens the last sale. Sold now, Next lot (refused while a bid stands; unbid lots come back later), reorder (every lot once), End auction (unsold lots stay unsold).
- Enter-results mode: the commissioner types buyer (member or a typed name) + amount per lot, can change/remove; every change in the history. Locks the rules at the first entry; closes itself when every lot is in (still changeable from the board: "Change who bought what").

### Money (cents, information only)

- Payout rows: each win (n slots), reaches round r (n slots), champion, biggest win margin (weekend games), place (golf). Every slot = floor(pool x pct); leftover cents go to the bigger percentages first, so slots sum exactly to the pool. Slots are paid in time order of the results; more results than slots are flagged and get $0 (never over-paid). Earnings of unsold lots are shown as "unclaimed" for the commissioner.
- Series leagues (NBA/NHL/MLB): a "win" = a series won (counted once). NFL playoffs: ESPN postseason weeks 1/2/3/5 -> rounds 1-4 (byes reach round 2 without a win). Hand results override the feed per team.

### Formats and rules (server-enforced)
- Pickleball: singles / doubles / mixed (also teams for other sports); games to 11, 15 or 21, win by 2 (past the target a game ends exactly 2 ahead); best of 1/3/5; rally or side-out is a label only; seeding by typed rating (e.g. DUPR, display only), by the unofficial Oddable rating (stand-in hook `D.oddRating`, falls back to the typed number), random, or as entered. Other sports: points to win 0 (= highest score wins, e.g. bowling) to 999, win by 1 or 2.
- Round robin: circle method, odd counts get one bye per round (each entry exactly once), matches spread over courts in waves, 1-8 pools (snake seeding). Pool tiebreaks: wins > head-to-head among the tied (mini-table) > point differential > points scored > seed. Optional single-elimination playoff of the top 2/4/8/16 (pool winners first), created when the last pool match is confirmed.
- Single elimination: standard seed order, top seeds get byes. Double elimination: losers bracket with drop-ins flipped on alternate rounds; grand final; bracket reset only if the losers-bracket champion wins the first final (property-tested 2-16 entries: always finishes, every non-champion has exactly 2 losses).
- Ladder: challenge up to N spots above (1-10), one open challenge per entry, play within 1-30 days, else forfeit win for the challenger or cancelled (commissioner's choice); winner swaps places or takes the spot and pushes the rest down.
- Heads-up: challenge → the challenged side accepts / declines → best of 1/3/5. Side matches (heads-up next to any event) are optional and do not change standings.
- Solo / skills (also one-player practice): one number per entry, highest or lowest wins (golf putting = strokes, lowest).
- Bracket pick'em (spectators and players, round robin / single / double elim): a whole round locks for picking when its first match starts (`live` tap) or gets a score (review fix 6; there is no scheduled match time); others' picks are hidden, then shown as counts only after the lock; 1 point per right pick (or later rounds worth more); tiebreak = closest guess of the final's total points, then who finished picking first.
- Scores: the designated scorer (first-listed side / either side / commissioner only) enters game scores, the other side confirms or disputes (with a note); disputes and scores against guests with no account go to the commissioner, who enters the right score (final). Every entry, confirm, dispute, correction, forfeit, draw and join is in the audit trail (last 400; screen shows 60). Op ids make offline resends safe; `v` per match rejects a stale screen (409).
- Rules and entries lock at the start or at an early draw (the draw is made automatically at the start). Money: entry, main/side pot, split, commissioner's take (none / 0-50 % / flat) are information only.
- People cap: stand-in `limits.maxPeople(accountKey, gameId)` (8 unless the account is enterprise; enterprise = env `ENTERPRISE_MAX_PEOPLE`, default 500, same as the Groups branch) and the shared text `limits.ENTERPRISE_TEXT`. Full games answer 403 with that text.
- Every match stores who played on each side at the time (`m.pp`, both doubles partners) and every game score; guests have their own ids (`extra.guestId`) and can claim their history later with a claim code.
