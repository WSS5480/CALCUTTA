### The identity / bets design (and why)
1. **A new account is still a cell number + PIN account, and the email + password is ATTACHED to it** (My Apps `link`, two steps). **Cell number + PIN stay REQUIRED at sign-up** (owner said "PIN option"; this is the coder's decision, owner to confirm). Why: the bets can only be stored in the row keyed by the cell number, the My Apps account id is made from that row id, and the dashboard / My Apps feed / Groups / bet alerts / events find a player through it. An account with no row would have nowhere to keep bets and would not show up as a player. The sign-up screen says it in one line: "Your cell number and PIN are needed: your bets are saved under them, and they sign you in fast on this phone. Your email and password get you back in if you lose the PIN."
2. **`/acct/login` uses `account` from My Apps' answer** (contract 5i), accepted only when it is the typed email or a phone account id. So an email + password sign-in of a linked account gets the SAME token identity as the cell number + PIN.
3. **A device signed in by email gets the bets WITHOUT typing the PIN: `/sync/pull|login|push` also accept `{token}` in the body** (no new header; the `/sync` CORS list is unchanged). `sync.js tokenRow()`: the token must be one `acct.js` signed (`tokenAcct`: gate ON, good signature, not expired, not the owner-PIN token, not signed before the account's last reset), the account must be a phone account, and its row is found from the account id (`rowIdFor`: `/acct/phone` notes the pair on every sign-in; otherwise one read of the row ids, at most once in 30 s for a miss). Why this does not weaken the rows: a token is only ever issued after the PIN was checked, after My Apps accepted an email + password whose address was PROVEN by an emailed code (build .40), or after a recovery code; a token opens only its own account's row; with the gate off (signing key guessable) no token is accepted at all; a body that carries a phone + PIN is still the PIN path (a token never rescues a wrong PIN); `/sync/signup` is not open to tokens. The PIN path itself is byte for byte what it was (the push loop moved into `pushRow()` unchanged).
4. **A PIN change keeps the bets because the PIN was never part of the key.** `sync.setPin(id, pin)` is ONE write on the row that sets only `salt` and `pinhash` (new random salt). The row id, `data` and `updated` are not in the write, so a push landing at the same moment cannot be lost, nothing is re-keyed, and the My Apps account and its made-up password are the same as before. The old PIN stops working at once (its hash no longer matches).
5. **Every device is signed out after a reset.** `acct.revoke(account)` stamps "now"; `verify()` refuses that account's tokens issued before the stamp (issue time = `x` − 30 days). The stamps live in the row `lock:pinrev` of `oddable_sync` (the `lock:` prefix keeps it out of every player list; tested) and are re-read at most once a minute, so a restart does not forget them. `/acct/me` answers such a token with `401 {code:"resign"}`; the phone then asks to sign in again and **clears nothing** (`reauth()` in gate.js; an ordinary 401 still signs out and wipes as before). A phone that still has a working PIN saved (only the password was reset) signs itself back in without a word.
6. **Same person, two ways in:** `oddOwner(id, acct)` now also takes the account id, so signing in by email on a phone that was signed in by cell number (same account) does not wipe the phone.

1. **Formats**: stroke play gross, stroke play net, Stableford (net points: 2 for a net par, +1 per stroke under, -1 per stroke over, never below 0). Skins and match play later.

7. **Ties** share a place ("T2", the next place is skipped). No count-back.
8. **Unfinished cards** show "Thru N" and are ranked by score to par (Stableford: points so far). The commissioner closes the day; an 18-hole day may be closed as the front 9 or the back 9 only when EVERY player has all nine of those holes, and then only those holes count.
9. **Joining** until the first score is logged. The commissioner may remove a member before the first score. Players are members of the group, so never more than the group's member limit.
10. Signed-in members of the group only. No typed-in guests.
11. One open Golf day per group at a time.
12. A member may change his own hole until the day is closed (every change is logged).

### Coder's decisions inside those rules (manager / owner to confirm)
- **Strokes by hole**: the playing strokes are dealt by stroke index over the holes in play: every hole gets floor(strokes / holes) and the (strokes mod holes) hardest holes one more. On a 9-hole day the typed number is for those nine. A day SHORTENED to nine keeps the strokes each hole already had (nothing is re-dealt after play).
- **A Golf day "starts" when the commissioner makes it** (that is when My Apps is told). Scores open on the day of the round (Chicago date); before it "Log my score" is replaced by "Scores open on the day of the round, <date>".
- **A member may re-type his own playing strokes, or leave, before the first score** (the re-type is logged). After the first score nobody's number moves.
- **The commissioner corrects only a hole the member has entered** (he cannot enter a first score for someone, and cannot wipe one). A member may also clear his own hole (logged).
- **Cancel** = a day with no score (it disappears). **Close** = a day with at least one score. A closed day cannot be reopened or changed by anyone. The open day and the last 5 closed days are kept.
- **The map's stroke indexes are prefilled too** when the map has them and the commissioner has typed none (rule 3 says "typed by the commissioner": he sees them in the boxes, is told to check them, and can change every one).
- **The course is not re-checked against the map by the server**: it stores the id and name the commissioner picked from the search (the id must look like a map id, `way/123`).
